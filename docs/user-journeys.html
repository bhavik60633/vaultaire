00

The big picture

Your vault is always in one of two states: locked or open. Everything else is a way between them.

Your fingerprintOpens the vault every day. Nothing to type.
Your vault passwordThe backup way in, and needed for anything that weakens your protection.
Your Recovery Key28 characters you write down once, on paper. With your password, it gets you back in if the phone's keys are lost.

Changed your fingerprints? If you add or remove a fingerprint in your phone's settings, fingerprint unlock turns itself off. Type your vault password once and it turns back on. This stops someone from adding their own finger to get in.

Finding things

The home screen is one page of sections. Cards always come first; you choose the order of the rest.

Home screenGreeting, a "Protections on" line, search, then your sections: Cards, Passwords, API keys, Bank accounts, Identity, Notes, Memberships.
Browse

Tap an itemOpens its details. Cards show the card face and finish.

Type in searchSearches titles and names across every category, hidden ones included. Results stay grouped by category.
Tap a resultOpens its details.
Organise
"Organise sections"At the bottom of home, or Settings → Appearance.
Move sections up or down, or hide themCards stay pinned first. Changes apply at once; "Reset to default" undoes them.

Hiding is tidying, not protection. A hidden section's items are still in your vault, still found by search, still in your backups, and listed at the bottom of home ("Hidden from home: API keys (2)").

Backups and a new phone

Your vault exists only on this phone. A backup file is how it survives a lost or broken phone. You're reminded every 30 days.

Make a backup
Settings → Create encrypted backup
Enter your vault password
Choose where to save itYour cloud drive, a USB stick, your computer. Vaultaire never uploads anything itself.
Backup file savedOpening it needs your vault password and your Recovery Key. The file alone is useless to anyone.
Restore on a new phone
Install Vaultaire → "Restore from backup"
Pick the backup file
Type your Recovery KeyTypos are caught at once. A key from a different backup is caught too.
Type your vault passwordIf anything is wrong: "Wrong password or Recovery Key, or the file is damaged."
correct
Check what's in it, then confirm
Vault restoredTurn fingerprint unlock back on. Your paper Recovery Key still works. You're prompted to delete the vault from the old phone.

Anything added after your last backup is not in it. Merging a backup into an existing vault is also possible: imported items are added as new and tagged "Imported" with the date. Nothing gets overwritten.

I forgot my password and my fingerprint doesn't work
The vault cannot be openedNot by you, not by us, not by anyone. We never hold a copy of your keys, and there is no support-desk reset.
If you have a backupYou still need the password to open it. The periodic password check exists to stop this from happening.

We will never ask for your Recovery Key. You only ever type it into Vaultaire, in restore and reset screens. Anyone else asking for it is trying to steal your vault.

Changing settings

Making protection stronger is free. Making it weaker always needs your vault password.

Stronger: no password
ExamplesShorter auto-lock, more frequent password checks, turning on password-only mode, hiding or reordering sections.
Weaker: password first
ExamplesLonger auto-lock, turning off fingerprint for critical fields, changing your password, exporting a backup, regenerating the Recovery Key.

Device protection check (Settings) shows what could weaken your phone's safety: no screen lock, an old security patch, USB debugging left on, a third-party keyboard, or no recent backup.

10

What Vaultaire never does

  • Connect to the internet. The app has no network permission at all.
  • Sync, upload, or keep an account for you.
  • Let your phone PIN alone open the vault.
  • Reset your vault through support, email or security questions.
  • Ask for your Recovery Key.
  • Store a card PIN.
  • Store a CVV unless you switch it on for that card.
  • Allow screenshots or screen recording of the app.
  • Show vault items in notifications, widgets or phone search.
  • Include analytics, ads or crash-reporting services.

Unaudited beta. Until an independent security review is published, don't store real credentials in Vaultaire.
